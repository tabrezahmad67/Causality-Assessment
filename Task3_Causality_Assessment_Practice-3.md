# Task 3: Causality Assessment

## Practice Case from the Tutorial

**Assessment/report date: 04-Oct-2026**

On 01-Jun-2021, Raj was started on amoxicillin 500 mg three times a day for a throat infection. On 02-Jun-2021, he developed itchy rashes over his body. On 03-Jun-2021, the dose was reduced to 250 mg three times a day, and the rash disappeared on 04-Jun-2021. As the intensity of the infection increased, the dose was increased to 500 mg three times a day on 05-Jun-2021. On 06-Jun-2021, he again developed generalized itchy rashes.
